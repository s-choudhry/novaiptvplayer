Nova IPTV Player Support

Help, questions, purchase support, restore support, and bug reports for Nova IPTV Player.

Nova IPTV Player is a media player for macOS. The app does not provide, host, sell, or distribute TV channels, movies, series, playlists, or IPTV subscriptions. Users must provide their own IPTV provider account, playlist, or stream source.

Contact Support

Email: novaiptvplayer@gmail.com

When contacting support, include as much of the following as possible:

  • App version.
  • macOS version.
  • Device model, such as MacBook Pro or Mac mini.
  • A short description of what you were trying to do.
  • The exact error message, if any.
  • Whether the problem happens every time or only sometimes.
  • Whether the issue is with app launch, portal setup, playback, purchase, restore, trial, or settings.

Do not send IPTV passwords unless support specifically asks for a temporary test account. Never send payment card details.

App Store Purchase or Restore

Use Unlock to purchase the full app through Apple In-App Purchase. Use Restore if you already purchased the app using the same Apple ID.

If purchase or restore does not work:

  1. Confirm the Mac is signed into the correct Apple ID.
  2. Confirm the Mac has an active internet connection.
  3. Quit and reopen Nova IPTV Player.
  4. Try Restore again.
  5. Contact support with your app version and macOS version.

Trial

The Mac App Store version includes a local 7-day trial. After the trial ends, playback requires the App Store unlock purchase.

IPTV Provider or Playlist Problems

Nova IPTV Player connects to the IPTV provider or playlist that you configure. If channels, movies, series, EPG, or account login do not work, first confirm that your IPTV provider account is active and that the same URL/credentials work in another player.

Playback Problems

If playback is black, jerky, buffering, or fails to start:

  1. Try another channel or video.
  2. Check your internet connection.
  3. Restart the app.
  4. Restart the Mac.
  5. Contact support with the stream type and app version.

Privacy Policy

Support Reports

The app can create an encrypted support report for troubleshooting. Only create and send a support report if you want support to inspect the diagnostic information. The report is encrypted before it is saved.

Response Time

Support requests are normally reviewed as soon as possible. Response time can vary depending on the issue and the amount of information provided.
